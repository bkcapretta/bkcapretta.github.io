## Ballot Check - Wisconsin
**Date:** Fall 2020

I designed and developed the first iteration of the website **Ballot Check** to educate Wisconsin voters about the representatives they're voting for in their district's ballot. This website provides the steps necessary to vote and educates voters on the election's key issues, like COVID-19, the economy, healthcare, criminal justice, and reproductive rights. In just a team of two engineers, we used the Cicero API within WIX to pull the correct polling and district information and display to users. Check it out [here](https://www.thedownballot.org/)!

## Budget Manipulator
**Date:** Spring 2018

Developed a multi-faceted [budget sheet](https://github.com/bkcapretta/budget_manipulator) using Google Sheets' API which allows one to flex and manage salaries. The sheet triggers upon edit to adjust the percent allocated per salary or yearly rate while maintaining an overall budget.
